    how this is scored

    100 − 100 × checked weight ÷ total weight. Bars run the same sum per category.

    Weight is based on the question, on a 0.4 to 6 ladder. Consensual kink caps at 2.5.

    So a single heavy question drops the score further than several light ones, and no category is scored against any other.
