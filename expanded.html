    how this is scored

    Every question carries a weight: 0.4 trivial, 0.8 mild, 1.5 notable, 2.5 heavy, 4 extreme, 5 criminal, 6 felonious. Your score is 100 − round(100 × checked weight ÷ total weight), so checking one felonious item costs about fifteen times what a trivial one does. Category bars run the same maths over just that category.
