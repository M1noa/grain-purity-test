    how this is scored

    Weight comes from the question, never from the category it sits in. The ladder runs 0.4, 0.8, 1.5, 2.5, 4, 5, 6, so the heaviest item costs fifteen times the lightest.

    What moves a question up that ladder is harm to someone else. Consensual kink therefore caps at 2.5 however extreme it gets, and the one 4 in that category is breaking a negotiated limit. Things that happened to you rather than things you chose sit near the floor: being robbed, catching an STI, having a nude passed around. Doing outweighs watching. Where a cluster overlaps, the umbrella carries the weight and the specifics are small top-ups, so one relationship is not billed three times.

    Your score is 100 − 100 × checked weight ÷ total weight, shown to two decimals. The bars run the same sum over one category at a time.
